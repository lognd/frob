---
id: INV-032
statement: A ticket chained via blocked_by to an open blocker never
  appears in frob ticket doable until that blocker resolves -- a
  blocked_by chain (e.g. roadmap phase parents) always surfaces work in
  proof-dependency order
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/test_tickets.py::TestDoable::test_blocked_excluded
  - tests/test_tickets.py::TestDoable::test_planned_included
---

`_doable_candidates` (feeding `doable`) filters queued/planned tickets to
those with no OPEN blocker (`_open_blockers`) -- a ticket whose
`blocked_by` names a still-open ticket id is excluded from the doable
list entirely, regardless of its own state. This is the general
mechanism the roadmap's phase-chained `blocked_by` structure relies on:
a phase's epic ticket only becomes doable once its parent phase's exit
criterion ticket closes.

`test_blocked_excluded` proves a ticket blocked by an open ticket is
excluded while the blocker remains open; `test_planned_included` proves
the positive case (no open blocker) is not accidentally excluded too.
