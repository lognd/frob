id: INV-029
statement: A discharge claim evaluated below its weakness catalog entry's
  required evidence rung is a violation, never silently accepted as
  sufficient
criticality: high
evidence:
  - tests/unit/strata/test_threat.py::TestDischargeCompleteness::test_discharge_claim_below_required_rung_is_a_violation
---

`_check_discharge_shape_and_rung` (one of `check_discharge_completeness`'s
THREAT003 gates) checks a firing weakness obligation's discharging claim
against the catalog entry's required evidence rung (`require proof >=
Ln`). A claim whose evaluated verdict rung is weaker than required --
e.g. an L1 example test standing in for an L4 universal-policy
requirement -- is reported as a violation rather than accepted as if it
were sufficient; evidence.md's "evidence weaker than the required rung
fails the SYS gate" rule is this check.

`test_discharge_claim_below_required_rung_is_a_violation` constructs
exactly that under-strength claim against a catalog entry demanding a
higher rung and asserts `check_discharge_completeness` reports it.
