---
id: INV-027
statement: Every synchronous flow into a crashable node must declare a
  timeout at least as long as the node's restart+retry bound, unless the
  flow is explicitly exempted (e.g. async) -- the no-hang check
criticality: high
evidence:
  - tests/unit/strata/test_crash.py::TestNoHangCheck::test_missing_timeout_into_crashable_node_fails_closed
  - tests/unit/strata/test_crash.py::TestNoHangCheck::test_timeout_shorter_than_restart_bound_fails_closed
  - tests/unit/strata/test_crash.py::TestNoHangCheck::test_timeout_covering_restart_plus_retry_bound_passes
  - tests/unit/strata/test_crash.py::TestNoHangCheck::test_async_flow_is_exempt_from_the_no_hang_check
---

`_validate_no_hang_flow` fails closed (`Err(StrataError.MissingTimeout)`)
when a synchronous flow into a crashable node declares no timeout at all,
and fails closed again (`Err(StrataError.IncompatibleTimeout)`) when a
declared timeout is shorter than the node's crash-contract restart+retry
bound (`_crash_bound_seconds`). A caller can plausibly hang forever
waiting on a crashed dependency's recovery -- the no-hang check (T-0074)
makes that a modeled elaboration error rather than a silent gap. An async
flow is exempt by design (a queue-based caller does not block).

`test_missing_timeout_into_crashable_node_fails_closed` and `test_
timeout_shorter_than_restart_bound_fails_closed` prove both failure
directions; `test_timeout_covering_restart_plus_retry_bound_passes` proves
the passing case; `test_async_flow_is_exempt_from_the_no_hang_check`
proves the declared exemption actually exempts.
