---
id: INV-014
statement: A call to a PUBLIC symbol is never recorded as a call-graph
  edge; only private/same-file callees are, so closure stops at the
  public-API boundary automatically
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/test_dup_inline.py::TestCallGraphBounds::test_public_callee_never_becomes_an_edge
  - tests/test_dup_inline.py::TestCallGraphBounds::test_call_edge
---

`build_call_graph` only records an edge for a call resolved to a PRIVATE
(leading-underscore) or same-file callee. A call to a public symbol is
never added to the graph at all -- there is no separate bookkeeping step
that filters public callees out afterward; they simply never enter the
edge set, which is what makes `closure`'s bounded BFS stop at the
public-API boundary for free.

`test_public_callee_never_becomes_an_edge` builds a call graph over a
fixture with a call to a public symbol and asserts no edge exists for it;
`test_call_edge` proves the positive case (a private/same-file callee
does get an edge), confirming the filter is directional, not an accident
of an empty graph.
