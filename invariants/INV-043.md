T-0682's incident: `splice_ledger`'s `_newer` comparator carries a subtle
ORDERING property (a Done-report side only wins over a reportless side
when the reportless side does not strictly outrank it) that a first-pass
fix got wrong in the OPPOSITE direction from the bug it was fixing --
twice, in the field, because the property lived only in a reviewer's
head. T-0757 turns "this property must be established, and stay
established" into a gate: `frob:invariant INV-043 establishes="..."`,
anchored on `_newer` in `src/frob/tickets/_land.py`, requires a
`frob:tests ... kind="property"` edge (INV008) reaching that anchor --
a bare fixed-input example test does not satisfy it. The bound evidence
here is a Hypothesis property test
(`TestNewerWinnerQualifiedPreferenceProperty`, `tests/test_ticket_land.py`)
proving both halves of the qualified rule (terminal supremacy, and the
richer-vs-strictly-outranking-poorer tiebreak) over the small state space
`_newer_winner` actually discriminates on, exhaustively rather than
case-by-case.
