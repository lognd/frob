`docs/modules/cli.md`'s "Generated command reference" section claims the
block between its two marker comments is written by `frob docs
--sync-commands` from the live top-level argparse registry and "never
hand-edited". `frob.gates._docblocks.generate_cli_command_table`/
`sync_cli_command_table` (T-1011) is what actually produces and
regenerates that text, and `doc005_gate` is what enforces the claim:
`test_doc005_freshness_flags_stale_generated_block` proves a stale row
left in the doc fires an ERROR-severity DOC005 finding naming the file,
and `test_doc005_freshness_passes_after_sync` proves the same fixture
clears once `sync_cli_command_table` has run -- the claim is checked at
byte-freshness, not merely asserted in prose.
