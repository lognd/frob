`check_catalog_completeness` (THREAT001) checks every CWE named by a
selected baseline view (e.g. `owasp-top-10`) against the loaded
`CWE_CATALOG`: a CWE with neither a catalog `weakness` entry nor a
matching `out-of-scope CWE-### reason="..."` declaration is reported as a
violation naming the CWE. An unaddressed baseline CWE cannot pass
silently -- it is either covered, explicitly excused with a reason, or a
gate finding.

`test_missing_entry_is_a_violation` proves a catalog thinned by one CWE
against a baseline that requires it fires exactly one violation naming
that CWE; `test_out_of_scope_entry_excuses_a_missing_catalog_entry`
proves the explicit excuse path actually suppresses it;
`test_full_catalog_satisfies_owasp_top_10_view` proves the real shipped
catalog is currently complete against the real baseline view.
