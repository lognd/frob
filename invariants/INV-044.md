`docs/modules/release.md`'s REL002 section calls `.frob-release.json`'s
`version` "the ONE authority" other release artifacts must match --
`frob.gates._rel002_coherence_violations` (T-1009) is what actually
enforces this, comparing it against `pyproject.toml`'s `[project].version`
and `uv.lock`'s own package-version entry and reporting an ERROR-severity
REL002 finding naming every disagreeing artifact. Checked, not merely
asserted: `test_clean_repo_has_no_rel002` proves a repo that has run
`frob release sync` has zero disagreements, and the other two prove a
hand-edited `pyproject.toml`/`uv.lock` (bypassing `sync`) each
independently trip REL002.
