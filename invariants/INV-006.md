---
id: INV-006
statement: An ARCH001 ceiling= waiver stops covering a function once it
  grows past the declared ceiling; it does not mute the finding forever
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/test_arch_gate.py::TestArchGateWaivers::test_ceiling_refires_when_grown_past_it
---

`frob:waive ARCH001 reason="..." ceiling="N"` is a reasoned "this long
function is justified up to N lines" escape, not a blanket permanent mute.
`_match_waiver` only honors the waiver while `_ceiling_ok` holds
(`violation.metric <= ceiling`); once the function's line count exceeds
the declared ceiling, the waiver stops matching and ARCH001 fires again on
that site. Without this, a waiver written for a 120-line function would
silently keep covering it after an unreviewed regrowth to 400 lines,
turning a bounded exception into an unbounded one.

`test_ceiling_refires_when_grown_past_it` grows a waived function past its
declared ceiling and asserts the gate reports the violation again rather
than staying silent.
