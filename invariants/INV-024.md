---
id: INV-024
statement: frob ticket doable excludes, by default, any candidate whose
  declared scope overlaps an in-progress ticket's active scope-lease --
  two agents dispatched straight off doable can never collide on files
criticality: high
evidence:
  - tests/test_tickets_lease.py::TestDoable::test_real_collision_is_hidden_from_default_doable
  - tests/test_tickets_lease.py::TestDoable::test_two_precise_in_progress_tickets_do_not_over_hide
  - tests/test_tickets_lease.py::TestDoable::test_ignore_lease_returns_raw_list
---

`doable` filters its blocker-only candidate list through `leased_by`,
which checks whether a candidate's declared `scope` globs overlap any
currently in-progress ticket's active scope-lease. A candidate that would
collide is excluded from the default list entirely -- no hand-maintained
collision blocklist a coordinator would otherwise have to build and keep
current. `ignore_lease=True` (`--ignore-lease`) is the explicit opt-out
back to the raw, blocker-only list.

`test_real_collision_is_hidden_from_default_doable` proves a genuinely
overlapping candidate is hidden by default; `test_two_precise_in_
progress_tickets_do_not_over_hide` proves the filter is precise (it does
not over-exclude disjoint-scope candidates); `test_ignore_lease_returns_
raw_list` proves the escape hatch actually bypasses the filter when asked.
