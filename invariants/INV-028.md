---
id: INV-028
statement: worst_age never silently clamps a positive-age cycle reaching
  the target; it reports the staleness as unbounded (inf) with the
  cycle's witness path
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/unit/strata/test_facts.py::TestClosure::test_worst_age_reports_unbounded_on_a_positive_cycle
  - tests/unit/strata/test_facts.py::TestClosure::test_worst_age_accumulates_along_the_stalest_path
---

`FactBase.worst_age` computes the longest-path accumulated staleness
reaching a target over per-hop flow ages. When a positive-age cycle can
reach the target, the true worst case is unbounded -- `worst_age` returns
`inf` together with the cycle's witness path rather than picking an
arbitrary finite bound or silently truncating the search, which the claim
evaluator (`_claims.py`) turns into a REFUTED verdict with a real
counterexample rather than a false PROVED.

`test_worst_age_reports_unbounded_on_a_positive_cycle` constructs exactly
that cycle and asserts `inf` comes back with the cycle as the witness
path; `test_worst_age_accumulates_along_the_stalest_path` proves the
ordinary (acyclic) case picks the genuinely stalest path, not just any
path.
