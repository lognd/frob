---
id: INV-012
statement: FUZZ003 re-obligates a fuzz-obligated function only when its
  body digest moved since the last recorded run, never on wall-clock age
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/test_fuzz.py::TestFuzz003::test_flags_missing_stamp
  - tests/test_fuzz.py::TestFuzz003::test_flags_stale_stamp
  - tests/test_fuzz.py::TestFuzz003::test_passes_for_matching_stamp
---

The fuzz stamp (`load_fuzz_stamp`) records, per obligated target, the
body digest at the last completed budgeted fuzz run. `FUZZ003` compares
the target's CURRENT body digest (from the graph snapshot) against the
recorded one -- an untouched function (same digest) never re-obligates,
regardless of how much time has passed; a changed function (digest moved)
or one with no recorded stamp at all fires.

`test_flags_missing_stamp` proves a target with no stamp entry fires;
`test_flags_stale_stamp` proves a target whose current digest no longer
matches the recorded one fires; `test_passes_for_matching_stamp` proves a
target whose digest still matches the stamp is silent -- together these
confirm the check is digest-keyed, not time-keyed.
