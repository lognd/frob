---
id: INV-002
statement: The ticket state machine has no illegal transitions
criticality: high
evidence:
  - tests/test_tickets.py::TestStateMachine::test_legal_transitions
  - tests/test_tickets.py::TestStateMachine::test_illegal_transitions
---

`transition` is the only sanctioned way to move a ticket between states
(queued/planned/in-progress/blocked/done/dropped); every caller (`frob
ticket start`, `frob ticket done`, agents closing work) goes through it.
If an illegal edge were ever accepted -- e.g. `queued` straight to `done`,
skipping evidence and a done-report -- COV003 and the done-without-evidence
checks downstream would have nothing to verify against, silently breaking
the "done means proven" contract the whole gates system rests on.

<!-- frob:used-by src/frob/tickets/__init__.py -->
<!-- frob:used-by tests/test_tickets.py -->

`test_legal_transitions` parametrizes every edge the state machine
actually allows and asserts each succeeds; `test_illegal_transitions`
parametrizes every non-adjacent pair (queued->done, done->queued,
in_progress->planned, etc.) and asserts each returns
`Err(TicketError.InvalidTransition)`. Together the two parametrized tests
enumerate the full transition matrix, not just a spot check.
