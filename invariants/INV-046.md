---
id: INV-046
statement: A relative path= entry in fleet.toml always resolves against
  the manifest FILE's own directory, never the process cwd, regardless
  of where frob fleet is invoked from
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/unit/fleet/test_manifest.py::TestLoadManifest::test_load_manifest_ok
  - tests/unit/fleet/test_manifest.py::TestLoadManifest::test_relative_path_resolves_against_manifest_dir_not_cwd
---

`docs/modules/fleet.md`'s Manifest section claims a `fleet.toml`
committed at a repo root with `path = "../typani"` "always means the
sibling checkout next to this repo, regardless of where frob fleet
happens to be invoked from" -- `frob.fleet._rebase_relative_paths`
(called from `load_manifest`) is what actually resolves this.
`test_load_manifest_ok` proves the basic rebase; T-1023's new
`test_relative_path_resolves_against_manifest_dir_not_cwd` closes the
gap that test alone leaves open (its manifest dir and the process cwd
happen to coincide, so it cannot distinguish "rebased against manifest
dir" from "rebased against cwd") by chdir-ing somewhere else entirely
before loading and proving the resolved path still tracks the manifest
directory, not cwd.
