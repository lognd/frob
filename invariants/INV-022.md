---
id: INV-022
statement: Every telemetry free-text field is redacted through
  frob.gates._secrets's existing provider-pattern scanner before being
  written, never through a second hand-rolled scanner
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/test_telemetry.py::test_redact_command_hides_recognizable_secret
  - tests/test_telemetry.py::test_redact_command_leaves_ordinary_text_alone
---

`redact_command` is applied to every free-text field (command args, tool
inputs) a telemetry record stores before it is written to `.frob/`'s
local-only telemetry stream. It reuses `frob.gates._secrets`'s own
single-line scanner (`_scan_line`) rather than a second, independently
maintained pattern list -- a second scanner drifting out of sync with the
gate's own patterns would be worse than no scanner (a recognized-elsewhere
secret leaking into telemetry unredacted).

`test_redact_command_hides_recognizable_secret` proves a real
provider-shaped secret is redacted; `test_redact_command_leaves_ordinary_
text_alone` proves ordinary command text passes through unchanged, so the
scanner is not simply blanking everything.
