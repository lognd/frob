---
id: INV-018
statement: PERF005 fires on a recursive call with no provable termination
  measure and stays silent only when the function proves termination
  (structural descent, or a reasoned frob:invariant terminates directive)
criticality: medium
evidence:
  - tests/test_perf.py::test_perf005_fires_on_unproven_self_recursion
  - tests/test_perf.py::test_perf005_does_not_fire_on_structural_descent_with_guard
  - tests/test_perf.py::test_perf005_silenced_by_reasoned_termination_directive
---

PERF005 is sound, not a size-blind heuristic: a recursive call is
considered proven only when the function narrows its argument toward a
base case on a well-founded measure (a decreasing bounded integer or
structural descent with a guard), OR carries a `frob:invariant terminates
reason="..." measure="..."` directive (`_termination_reasoned`, requiring
both attributes non-empty). Anything short of that fires PERF005.

`test_perf005_fires_on_unproven_self_recursion` proves the negative case
(no proof at all -> fires); `test_perf005_does_not_fire_on_structural_
descent_with_guard` proves the automatic-proof path is recognized;
`test_perf005_silenced_by_reasoned_termination_directive` proves the
escape hatch actually silences the finding when used.
