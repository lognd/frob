# PM012 cycle-member-not-ready

A cycle member that has not started fails the definition of ready (`[pm] ready_requires`).

## What it does

Fires once per member of a planned or active cycle that is still in triage or todo and fails a predicate of `[pm] ready_requires` (`story_or_objective_qualified`, `criteria`, `points`, `scope`, `parent`). The message names every failed predicate. Members already in progress or done are not judged. An empty `ready_requires` turns the rule off.

## Why it matters

Pulling a ticket that lacks scope, acceptance or points leads to rework and unmeasurable commitments.

## Remedy

Complete the named fields with `frob ticket update TICKET`, or remove the ticket from the cycle with `frob cycle unassign TICKET`.

## Examples

### A violating case fires

```rust expect=fire file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```

### A conforming case is clean

```rust expect=clean file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```
