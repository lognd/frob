# PM010 cycle-over-capacity

A planned or active cycle commits more points than its capacity.

## What it does

Fires once per open cycle whose committed points (velocity-counting members only) exceed its capacity: the cycle's own `capacity_points`, else `rolling_mean - k * stddev` over closed cycles once `[pm] min_history` of them exist. Before that nothing is enforced and the rule is silent, so a fresh repository never trips it. The message carries the committed points and the capacity.

## Why it matters

`cycle assign` refuses past capacity unless `--over-commit --reason`, so a cycle over capacity got there by a hand edit, a merged ledger branch or a lowered capacity; commitments past what the team delivers are usually missed.

## Remedy

Move work to a later cycle with `frob cycle assign TICKET CYCLE`, raise the cycle's `capacity_points`, or record the choice with `--over-commit --reason WHY`.

## Examples

### A violating case fires

```rust expect=fire file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```

### A conforming case is clean

```rust expect=clean file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```
