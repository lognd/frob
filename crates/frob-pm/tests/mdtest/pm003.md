# PM003 story structure

`story KEY` adds a story; `as_a=`, `want=` and `so=` set its parts (underscores are spaces); `criteria=N` adds N Given/When/Then criteria.

## A complete story is clean

```text expect=clean
story a criteria=1
```

## A story without its value parts fires

```text expect=fire
story a value=none
```

## A one-word persona fires

```text expect=fire
story a as_a=maintainer
```

## A short so_that fires

```text expect=fire
story a so=faster
```

## A quality objective is exempt

```text expect=clean
story a value=none flavour=objective
```

## A done story is not examined

```text expect=clean
story a value=none state=done
```
