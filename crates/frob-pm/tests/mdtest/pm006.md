# PM006 story size

`story KEY` adds a story; `as_a=`, `want=` and `so=` set its parts (underscores are spaces); `criteria=N` adds N Given/When/Then criteria. `max_story_points N` sets the limit (default 8).

## A story at the limit is clean

```text expect=clean
story a points=8
```

## A story over the limit fires

```text expect=fire
story a points=13
```

## An unsized story is clean

```text expect=clean
story a
```

## A raised limit lets it through

```text expect=clean
max_story_points 13
story a points=13
```

## A limit of zero is off

```text expect=clean
max_story_points 0
story a points=13
```

## Unresolved when the ledger cannot be read

`unreadable` corrupts the ticket index after the ledger is built, so the rule cannot judge and reports the required Unresolved.

```text expect=unresolved
task a
unreadable
```
