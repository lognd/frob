# CI001 pinned-ref

An external `uses:` reference is not pinned to a full commit SHA.

## What it does

Bound to zizmor `unpinned-uses`.

## Why it matters

Unpinned or over-privileged workflows are a supply-chain and token-exposure risk, so the finding is raised where the workflow is declared.

## Remedy

Pin the action to its 40-hex commit SHA and keep the version in a trailing comment; Dependabot or Renovate can then bump both together. Raised by a `[[check.tool]]` stage with `parser = "zizmor-json-v1"`. Declared here until the grimble-ci crate owns the CI family; the id and meaning stay.

## Examples

### Fires case 1

```yaml expect=fire file=.github/workflows/ci.yml
jobs:
  b:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
```

### Clean case 1

```yaml expect=clean file=.github/workflows/ci.yml
jobs:
  b:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef01234567 # v4
```
