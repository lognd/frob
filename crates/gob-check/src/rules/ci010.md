# CI010 checkout-no-persist

`actions/checkout` leaves credentials in the workspace.

## What it does

Bound to zizmor `artipacked`.

## Why it matters

Unpinned or over-privileged workflows are a supply-chain and token-exposure risk, so the finding is raised where the workflow is declared.

## Remedy

Set `persist-credentials: false` on the checkout unless a later step must push. Raised by a `[[check.tool]]` stage with `parser = "zizmor-json-v1"`. Declared here until the grimble-ci crate owns the CI family; the id and meaning stay.

## Examples

### Fires case 1

```yaml expect=fire file=.github/workflows/ci.yml
jobs:
  b:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
```

### Clean case 1

```yaml expect=clean file=.github/workflows/ci.yml
jobs:
  b:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
        with:
          persist-credentials: false
```
