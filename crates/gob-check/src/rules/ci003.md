# CI003 no-top-level-write

A workflow or job grants a broader `GITHUB_TOKEN` scope than it needs.

## What it does

Bound to zizmor `excessive-permissions`.

## Why it matters

Unpinned or over-privileged workflows are a supply-chain and token-exposure risk, so the finding is raised where the workflow is declared.

## Remedy

Declare `permissions: contents: read` at the top of the workflow and widen single jobs only. Raised by a `[[check.tool]]` stage with `parser = "zizmor-json-v1"`. Declared here until the grimble-ci crate owns the CI family; the id and meaning stay.

## Examples

### Fires case 1

```yaml expect=fire file=.github/workflows/ci.yml
on: push
permissions: write-all
jobs: {}
```

### Clean case 1

```yaml expect=clean file=.github/workflows/ci.yml
on: push
permissions:
  contents: read
jobs: {}
```
