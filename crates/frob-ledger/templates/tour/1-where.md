## Stop 1: what lives where

This repository keeps two lines of history. The **code branch** holds the code. The **ticket branch** (this one) holds one file per ticket, the event log under `.events/`, and the generated pages such as the [front door](README.md), the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md), the [indexes](indexes/README.md) and the [guide](guide/README.md). Tickets are written only through `{{frob}} ticket ...`; generated pages are rewritten only by `{{frob}} ticket reindex`.
