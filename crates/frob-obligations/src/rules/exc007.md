# EXC007 defer-ticket-missing

A defer directive names a ticket that does not exist.

## What it does

The ticket resolves to nothing in the ledger. Not evaluated without a ledger.

## Why it matters

A defer pointing at nothing has no owner for its debt.

## Remedy

Fix the id or file the ticket that will pay the debt.

## Examples

### A violating case fires

```rust expect=fire file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```

### A conforming case is clean

```rust expect=clean file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```
