# EXC003 defer-ticket-terminal

A defer directive names a ticket that is done or dropped.

## What it does

The debt's ticket reached a terminal category, so the exit condition of the defer is met. The deferred finding stays suppressed and listed so the failure is this one finding, not a surprise.

## Why it matters

A defer that outlives its ticket hides debt nobody tracks.

## Remedy

Pay the debt (remove the directive and fix the finding) or re-point the defer at a live ticket.

## Examples

### A violating case fires

```rust expect=fire file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```

### A conforming case is clean

```rust expect=clean file=src/lib.rs
pub fn placeholder() {}
```
