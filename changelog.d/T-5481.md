Fixed by passing newline="" to write the diff bytes verbatim regardless of platform. Verified on the real Windows mirror (winrun) before (all 3 failed with "patch does not apply") and after (10/10 pass, confirmed on two consecutive Windows runs) plus 10/10 on Linux, no regressions.
